# Brand Visibility Intelligence Dashboard
Brand Visibility Intelligence Dashboard – Key Insights
1. Data Integration Insights
Successfully combined product data from multiple sources (API + CSV) into a unified dataset.
Standardized column names and data types to ensure consistency across platforms.
Created a consolidated product repository for cross-platform analysis.
2. Data Quality Insights
Missing values, duplicate records, and inconsistent formats were identified and corrected.
Price and review fields were converted to appropriate numeric formats for accurate calculations.
Text fields such as product names, brands, and platforms were standardized to improve reporting quality.
3. Product Portfolio Insights
The dashboard provides visibility into the total number of products tracked across platforms.
Product catalog size helps evaluate overall brand presence in the online marketplace.
Brands with larger product portfolios generally achieve higher search visibility.
4. Pricing Insights
Average product price indicates the brand's market positioning.
Maximum and minimum prices highlight premium and budget product segments.
Price distribution analysis helps identify pricing gaps and opportunities.
5. Rating Performance Insights
Average rating serves as a key indicator of customer satisfaction.
Products with consistently high ratings contribute positively to brand perception.
Low-rated products may require quality improvements or better customer support.
6. Customer Engagement Insights
Total reviews measure customer interaction and product popularity.
Products with higher review counts typically receive greater consumer trust.
High engagement products often achieve stronger marketplace visibility.
7. Discount Strategy Insights
Percentage of discounted products reveals promotional intensity.
Frequent discounting can increase visibility and sales volume.
Excessive discounts may impact profit margins and perceived product value.
8. Platform Performance Insights
Total platforms tracked indicate market coverage.
Best-performing platform (based on average rating) highlights where customers have the most positive experience.
Platform comparison helps prioritize marketing and inventory efforts.
9. Search Visibility Insights
Average product position measures how prominently products appear in search results.
Products appearing in top positions gain significantly higher customer attention.
Lower-ranked products may require SEO optimization, better content, or pricing adjustments.
10. Brand Visibility Score Insights
Visibility Score provides an overall measure of brand presence across platforms.
Higher visibility scores are generally associated with:
Better ratings
More reviews
Strong search rankings
Competitive pricing
This KPI helps track brand performance over time.
